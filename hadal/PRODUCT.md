# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/JS in `hadal/`, no build step or dependencies. Confirmed by the user. Lives beside the repo's existing practice page and does not touch it.

## Users

Inferred (the user delegated the theme): wealthy, curious adventurers who want an experience few humans have had, comparing a full-ocean-depth dive with other extreme travel such as suborbital flights or polar expeditions. They read the landing page at home before contacting the company.

## Product Purpose

HADAL is a fictional expedition company that takes small groups (one pilot and two passengers) in a crewed submersible to the Challenger Deep in the Mariana Trench, about 10,935 m down. The landing page has to make the descent feel real and get serious prospects to request an expedition briefing.

## Positioning

Inferred: HADAL sells the descent itself, hours of going from sunlight into total darkness, rather than luxury travel. The claim is about depth and physical truth: real ocean zones, real pressure, real darkness.

## Operating Context

Expeditions depart from a support ship over the trench. The dive takes about 4 hours down, a few hours on the floor and about 4 hours back up. Ocean zones on the way down: epipelagic (0–200 m), mesopelagic (200–1,000 m), bathypelagic (1,000–4,000 m), abyssopelagic (4,000–6,000 m) and hadalpelagic (6,000 m and deeper).

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Fictional brand. The scientific facts about depth, pressure, temperature and zones must be accurate.
- Undecided: price, dates, vessel name, crew names. Leave these out or show them as placeholders. Do not present invented figures as booking terms.
- Primary action: request an expedition briefing (application or contact). There is no real backend.

## Evidence on Hand

None. No testimonials, press, customers or photography. Do not fabricate testimonials, press logos, safety records or customer counts.

## Product Principles

1. The depth is the product. Every claim comes back to the descent.
2. Honest physics over hype. Real numbers do the persuading.
3. Scarcity through difficulty, not marketing tricks.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast on the dark palette. Motion respects `prefers-reduced-motion`.
