# HADAL landing

Scope: `hadal/index.html`, the one-page landing. Visitor mode: **Persuade**.

Audience and job: a wealthy, curious adventurer comparing extreme experiences decides whether to request an expedition briefing. Action: request the briefing. Proof: real ocean physics (zones, pressure, temperature, light, real deep-sea species, real historic dives). No testimonials, prices or dates. Constraint: the user pinned a dark palette.

Memorable moment: the visitor turns a paper crank and lowers a cut-paper submersible through five ocean zones. Each zone's paper creature moves only while the crank turns.

Responsive decisions: below 1180px the tab navigation is dropped. The page is one linear scroll and the briefing CTA stays pinned in the top bar. On phones the first-viewport budget goes to the headline and the crank diorama, with the email form below it.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The descent is a hand-cranked paper automaton. The visitor turns the crank and the ocean's procession passes the submersible, from sunlit turtle to hadal snailfish. This refuses the category default: an underwater video hero with a centred CTA and glowing particles.

OWN-WORLD: Cut and slotted paper on abyssal board instead of kraft. Board #0b1118 with fiber grain. Pulp-white paper #e9e3d6 for figures, tabs and labels, with paper grey and pressed ash as secondary stock. Cast shadows in shadow ink #020406. One crimson #c4282d, reserved for the figure part currently in motion, the crank knob and the visitor's commitment (true detail: red is the first color the sea swallows). Display type is Big Shoulders Stencil caps; labels are Big Shoulders Text caps; captions and readouts are Courier Prime set as workshop notes. Every control is a paper tab with notched corners and a slot mark.

STORY: The visitor learns that HADAL takes three people to 10,935 m. They believe it because the real physics change as they crank: pressure, temperature, light and real species. Then they request a briefing.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Tab navigation pinned across the top. On the left, five lines of stencil headline (max 6rem) with one crimson line "10.935 METROS", two lines of workshop caption, and a working email field with a crimson "SOLICITAR BRIEFING" tab. On the right, about 58% of the width is a paper diorama box: strata layers, the submersible hanging on its thread, the active creature with its shadow on the back wall. A title tab with live readouts (depth, pressure, temperature, light) sits above it, a crank on the box front, and a column of five zone keyframe tabs beside it.

FORM: Kinetic paper automata (catalog challenger kinetic-sculpture-automata-crank-paper-menagerie, adopted by the user from the declined row), position 2 of the dealt hand; seed key 1db03fa6. Signature interaction: a drag/keyboard crank as the single master clock. Motion grammar: linkages swing with the crank angle, strata translate, nothing animates on its own timer.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
